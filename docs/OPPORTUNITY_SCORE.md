# Opportunity score

Weekly contract weighting: pain 25%, willingness to pay 25%, evidence quality 20%, distribution fit 15%, CTA testability 15%. Each dimension is 0–5; weighted total is out of 100.

| Dimension | Score | Evidence and uncertainty |
|---|---:|---|
| Pain | 4 | Specific regret stories and costly first-year attrition suggest severe downside; frequency and avoidability are not measured. |
| Willingness to pay | 2 | YouScience anchors a $49 direct offer and schools buy per-seat tools, but no one has paid for this wedge. |
| Evidence quality | 3 | Search, CTE, competitor usage, and community receipts triangulate behavior; several figures are secondary and none validates this offer. |
| Distribution fit | 3 | Concentrated subreddits, practitioner creators, and CTE channels exist; school sales are slow and community promotion can be rejected. |
| CTA testability | 5 | A single scenario can ask for a $49 deposit or counselor pilot immediately with completion and conversion instrumentation. |

**Weighted total:** (4×25 + 2×25 + 3×20 + 3×15 + 5×15) / 5 = **66/100**. Confidence: medium-low. The score supports a narrow demand test, not a platform build.
