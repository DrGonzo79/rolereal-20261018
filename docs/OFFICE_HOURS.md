# Office Hours diagnostic

**Position:** do not build a 50-role school platform. Prove that one candid simulation changes a real decision and that a practitioner will sign it.

1. **Demand reality:** Forage usage, career-regret posts, CTE participation, and a viral job-game show attention. None proves payment for an “honest week.” The test must collect prepayment, not waitlist praise.
2. **Status quo:** prospects use quizzes, videos, Reddit, open days, informal shadowing, or commit to training blind. The cost is tuition, months/years, and avoidable first-year churn; exact prevented-loss value is unmeasured.
3. **Desperate customer:** a 24–35-year-old career changer about to pay for nursing school, scared of shift conditions and unable to access a shadow day. The school buyer is deliberately secondary.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** one named RN-signed night-shift simulation plus a 20-minute debrief for $49. No library, dashboard, LMS, AI authoring, or employer marketplace.
5. **Observation plan:** recruit 10 prospects from nursing/career-change communities; watch each complete it without guidance; capture pauses, abandoned choices, trust objections, and whether they book/prepay. Interview three RNs separately on accuracy and what feels unsafe or missing.
6. **Future-fit:** AI makes polished recruiter content cheaper, so trusted provenance and versioned practitioner accountability become more—not less—valuable. If users cannot distinguish signed reality from generic generated content, the thesis fails.

**Assignment:** secure one named RN reviewer and three $49 deposits before producing a second complete career.
