# Source research — 2026-10-18

## Report summary

- **Idea:** Career simulation platform that lets students try real jobs before committing
- **Public report:** https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/today
- **IB Index:** 7.3; marketplace
- **Potential:** $75K–$150K year one; $15M–$20M ARR ceiling (illustrative report estimates)
- **Reality:** moderate difficulty; $15K–$40K starting capital; 90–180 days to first revenue
- **Buyer/user:** CTE leaders and counselors buy; high-school/community-college students and adult career changers use; employers are later buyers
- **Painful status quo:** interest quizzes, recruiter-authored simulations, day-in-the-life video, and expensive real shadowing leave students without the unglamorous trade-offs of a job
- **Wedge:** one practitioner-signed “honest week,” starting with nursing, sold to the learner rather than shaped by a recruiter
- **Why now:** generative tooling lowers simulation production cost; districts are reconsidering incumbent career-planning vendors; first-year attrition remains expensive

## Evidence and market

The report cites 74K US monthly searches for the Forage brand (latest month 90.5K, $7.88 CPC) but only 110 for “virtual job simulations” and zero for two direct-solution phrases. This proves category behavior more than new-product intent. It also cites 301K/mo for Naviance, 135K for Xello, 22.2K for MajorClarity, and 18.1K for “career aptitude test,” with several declining year over year.

Demand signals include candid regret posts in nursing, teaching, skilled trades, and coding-bootcamp communities; 89% of US high-school counselors reporting CTE availability; 69% reporting work-based learning; Michigan reporting 55,431 CTE completers in 2024–25; and a solo-built Nigerian job-hunt game reportedly reaching 234,972 X views. These are directional signals, not purchase proof.

Competitors: Forage (free employer-funded simulations, 250+ simulations, 90+ employers, 10M+ engagements), Xello, SchooLinks, YouScience, Pathful, CareerVillage, Naviance, and free TikTok/YouTube content. The strongest incumbent response is Forage adding practitioner attribution.

## Offer and GTM

Offer ladder from the report: one free simulation for email; $49 role-specific career-changer kit with practitioner debrief; $5–$12/student/year school library plus reporting; later $500–$2,000 per hire. Report napkin math assumes 200K views → 1,000 emails (0.5%) → 20 kits (2%) = $980, plus one 400-student pilot at $8 = $3,200, for $4,180 in 90 days. Every conversion assumption remains unverified.

Initial distribution should be replies and useful free simulations in regret-heavy communities, practitioner co-distribution, CTE coordinator introductions, and short clips—not SEO. The first test is whether 10 nursing prospects complete a signed simulation and whether three will prepay $49 for the debrief kit.

## Risks and source domains

Main risks: free Forage bundle; zero direct-solution search; unproven practitioner supply; slow district procurement; student privacy/safety; simulations oversimplifying regulated work; buyer/user mismatch. Domains named in the report include Google Ads, Reddit, X, Rest of World, settlementinsight.com, Naviance/PowerSchool, Association for Career and Technical Education/NCES, and Michigan Department of Education. Raw report text and private tracking metadata are intentionally excluded.
